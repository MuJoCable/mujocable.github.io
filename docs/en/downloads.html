Source and Releases¶

  • SolidWorks reference-geometry exporter: define CAD landmarks, export CSV and bind MJCF sites; see the routing tutorial.

  • Public repository: source, build, tests, examples and licenses.

  • Releases: check OS, architecture and MuJoCo ABI for each actual binary. A source ZIP is not a prebuilt plugin.

  • Paper examples: frozen models, results and reproduction entry points.

  • Showcase examples: irregular pulleys and letter-mesh routes.

Bundles retain Apache-2.0 and relevant asset notices, including Faive attribution. Consult examples/paper/ASSET_SOURCES.md for third-party permissions; not every asset is an original MuJoCable design.
